Privacy Policy

Sensory SafeScape (Android app com.homilabs.safescape) by HomiLabs · Last updated 28 September 2026

Sensory SafeScape is made for children, so we collect as little as possible and never sell or share it. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking tools in the app.

What the app stores

DataWhyWhere
Child's nickname (optional) and age group (2–4, 5–7 or 8–10)To greet the child and suggest calm settingsDevice; cloud backup if on
Sensory settings (motion, lighting, sound filtering, colours)To keep the app comfortable for the childDevice; cloud backup if on
Routines (titles, pictures chosen from the app, short stories)To show step-by-step routinesDevice; cloud backup if on
Photos a grown-up adds to routine stepsTo show real places and objectsDevice only. Never uploaded.
Activity times (for example "Calming Canvas, 6 minutes, lavender colours"; routine steps completed)For the grown-ups' progress view and the optional PDF summaryDevice; cloud backup if on
A random account ID, and the parent's email only if a parent chooses to add oneTo keep the backup private and restore it on a new deviceGoogle Firebase

The app does not use the microphone or location, does not read contacts, does not record audio or video, and does not collect the child's real name, a photo of the child (unless a grown-up chooses one for a routine step, which stays on the device), or any advertising ID.

Cloud backup

By default, a private copy of the data above (except photos) is kept in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication) under a random anonymous ID. Only that account can read or write it. A grown-up can switch the backup off at any time in the app (Grown-ups area → Backup & account); the data then stays only on the device. Google processes this data on our behalf under the Firebase terms. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Permissions

The app only asks for internet access (for the optional cloud backup). Adding a photo to a routine uses Android's own photo picker or camera, so the app never gets access to your photo library or camera.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share data with anyone. The PDF summary is only created when a grown-up taps "Export", and it is only shared where the grown-up chooses (for example with a therapist).

Children

The app is designed for children under the supervision of a parent, carer or therapist, and follows Google Play's Families Policy. Settings, account options, links and sharing are behind a question young children can't answer. No personal information is collected from the child beyond the optional nickname entered by a grown-up.

Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept until you delete it. In the app: Grown-ups area → Backup & account → Delete account and all cloud data removes everything from the device and the cloud immediately. You can also remove a single child in the Children tab. If you no longer have the app, see how to delete your data.

Your rights

You can ask us what data we hold about you, ask for a copy, correct it, or have it deleted, by emailing homilabs.smc@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
