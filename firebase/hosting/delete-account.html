Delete your Sensory SafeScape account and data

App: Sensory SafeScape by HomiLabs (com.homilabs.safescape)

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open SafeScape and tap the lock at the top-left.
  2. Answer the grown-up question.
  3. Open Backup & account and tap Delete account and all cloud data.

This permanently deletes every child profile, routine, photo and activity record from the device and from our cloud backup, and closes the account.

Without the app

Email homilabs.smc@gmail.com with the subject "SafeScape data deletion", from the email address linked to your SafeScape backup. We delete the account and all its data within 30 days and confirm by email.

If you never linked an email, your backup is anonymous: uninstalling the app removes the copy on the device, and the anonymous cloud copy cannot be linked to you. You can still ask us to delete it by sending the Account ID shown in the app (Grown-ups area → Backup & account).

What is deleted

Nicknames, age groups, sensory settings, routines, activity times and the account (including the email, if linked). Nothing is kept afterwards, apart from what we must keep by law (normally nothing for this app).
