# Sensory SafeScape v1.2.0 (versionCode 4): scope and locked decisions

Agreed with the owner on 4 Nov 2026: add **both** "Learn with Sammy" (Calm Skills chapters) and **Picture Stories**. v1.1.0 is live, so this release is v1.2.0, versionCode 4 (code 3 was built but replaced on 5 Nov by a build with the grown-ups gate fix for 320 dp phones). Everything in `docs/design-review-v1.md` §0 still applies (errorless, English, local-first, no tracking, code-drawn Sammy, Noto pictograms).

Full child-facing wording for review: `docs/v1.2-content-review.md` (generated from the content files by `python3 scripts/content_review.py`).

## Locked decisions

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| V1 | New hub card **Learn with Sammy**: 8 chapters (My Body Talks, Feelings Have Names, My Calm-Down Tools, Too Loud Too Bright, When Plans Change, Asking for Help, Waiting My Turn, Playing Together). Each has 5–6 picture cards, read aloud, then one practice step. | Teaches the self-regulation and self-advocacy skills the rest of the app uses. |
| V2 | Practice steps are **errorless**: *choose* (any answer gets a warm reply), *breathe* (3 slow breaths, 4 s in / 6 s out), *squeeze* (press-and-hold ball, progressive relaxation), *count* (5-4-3 grounding with stars). A **Next** button is always available; nothing is marked wrong. | §0 L13. |
| V3 | **All chapters are open.** The suggested next chapter glows and finished chapters get a star. (This replaces the "unlock in order" idea from the proposal: locking content would block a child who needs a specific topic today.) | A child in distress shouldn't be told "not yet". |
| V4 | New hub card **Picture Stories**: 12 built-in first-person stories for real situations (airplane, new school, fire drill, vaccine, guests, Eid, power cut, long car trip, new baby, thunder, wedding, losing a game), page by page, read aloud. | Preparation for unpredictable or overwhelming events. |
| V5 | Parents can write **their own stories** (Grown-ups → Stories): title, cover picture, pages with text and a pictogram or their own photo. Stored per child like routines; mirrored to Firestore under `children/{id}/stories/{id}` **without photos** (photos stay on the device, §0 L6). | Personalised stories about the child's real places work best. |
| V6 | The term "Social Stories" is not used (it is Carol Gray's trademark). The feature is "Picture Stories". | Trademark. |
| V7 | Content lives in `assets/content/lessons.json` and `assets/content/stories.json`, loaded at start-up; tests check every pictogram exists and every chapter/story is well formed. | One source for the app, the review document and the tests. |
| V8 | Progress: lesson visits are sessions with mode `lesson` (`sound` = chapter id, cards seen / total), story readings mode `story` (`sound` = story id, pages read / total). Finished chapters are also kept on the child (`lessons_done`). The Progress tab and the OT PDF gain a **Learning** section (chapters finished, stories read, favourites). | Same reporting model as routines. |
| V9 | New pictograms: 39 more Noto Emoji (Apache 2.0), bringing the total to 100. No syringe picture: the vaccine story uses a plaster on its cover so a needle-anxious child isn't alarmed before the story starts. | Sensory and anxiety safety. |
| V10 | No new permissions, no new SDKs, no new audio files (completion uses the existing chime and done sounds; narration is the device text-to-speech). | Keeps the Data Safety form and size unchanged. |

## Out of scope for v1.2
Urdu narration or text (English is locked), recorded voices, video, quizzes or scores, unlocking or streaks.
